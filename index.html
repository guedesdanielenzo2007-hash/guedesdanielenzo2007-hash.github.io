Channel Uploader

Channel Uploader is a private tool used by a single creator to upload and schedule videos on their own YouTube channels: Canal Bia, Basia and Bounce Lab.

It runs only on the creator's own computer. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

It uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, titles, descriptions, translations and thumbnails to the channels that the owner authorized.
