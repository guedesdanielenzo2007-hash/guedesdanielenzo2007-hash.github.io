Privacy Policy – Channel Uploader

Last updated: October 29, 2026

What the app is

Channel Uploader is a private tool used by one creator to upload videos to their own YouTube channels (Canal Bia, Basia and Bounce Lab). It runs only on the owner's computer and has no other users.

Google data the app uses

The app requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload permission. It uses it to upload videos and their titles, descriptions, tags, translations and thumbnails to the authorized channel. It does not read your emails, contacts, files, watch history, comments or any other Google data.

Storage and sharing

  • The authorization token is stored only on the owner's computer and is never sent anywhere except to Google.
  • No personal data is collected, sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • The app does not use cookies, analytics or tracking.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file on the computer also removes the app's access.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the contact information on the "About" page of the YouTube channels listed above.
